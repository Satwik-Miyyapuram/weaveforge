      Debugging the KL collapse in exp-04

      Spent the whole day on this. Symptoms: from epoch 12 the KL term drops to ~0.02 nats and stays there, reconstruction keeps improving, MIG falls from 0.24 to 0.05. Classic posterior collapse, but only with the graph prior switched on — the β=4 baseline with the same seed is fine.

      What I ruled out, in order:

      1. Learning rate. Halved it (1e-3 → 5e-4): collapse moves to epoch 19, does not go away.
      2. KL warm-up. Linear warm-up over 10 epochs: same thing, just later.
      3. The decoder being too strong. Swapped the 4-layer deconv for the 2-layer one from the baseline: still collapses.
      4. Data. Re-ran the factor-count sanity check on the loader; splits are deterministic and identical to exp-02.

      What it was: the prior's variance head. The GIN readout feeds a softplus for σ, and with sum aggregation the pre-activation grows with node degree. High-degree concept nodes (#vae, #disentanglement) end up with σ in the hundreds, so the prior is nearly flat and the KL to it is tiny whatever the posterior does. The optimiser happily takes the free lunch.

      Fix: divide the readout by √degree before the softplus (the same normalisation GCN uses), and clamp log σ to [−5, 2]. Re-ran seed 7 for 20 epochs: KL settles at 9.8 nats, MIG 0.27 and climbing. Seeds 1 and 13 queued overnight.

      Lessons for the conventions page: log the prior's mean σ every epoch, not just the KL; a flat prior hides behind a healthy-looking loss curve. Also worth a sentence in §3.2 — anyone using sum aggregation in a prior network will hit this.

      Open question for the supervisor meeting: is the √degree normalisation a change to the method (needs an ablation row) or an implementation detail (footnote)? Leaning towards the ablation row, it is cheap.
