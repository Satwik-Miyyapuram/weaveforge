# Release notices

After an update, WeaveForge may show a short notice at the top of the app: a
title and a few lines on what changed. It appears on every screen until you
dismiss it.

## How to

1. Read the notice at the top of the app.
2. Click **Dismiss**. The notice hides at once.

## What to expect

| Case | What you see |
|---|---|
| A notice you have not dismissed | The newest one, as a banner above the screen |
| You dismissed it | It stays hidden, on this device and any other where you sign in |
| No notice, or it could not be loaded | No banner. Nothing else changes |

Notices are plain text. Only the newest unseen notice shows; older ones are
not queued behind it.
