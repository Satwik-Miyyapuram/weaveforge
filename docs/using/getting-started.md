# Getting started

Your first session takes about 10 minutes. You open WeaveForge, make a project,
add a paper, write your first note, and find it all again. You need the
[desktop app](desktop.md) installed, or the web app open in a browser.

## 1. Open the app

If you chose the desktop app, install it first. The [desktop page](desktop.md)
walks you through each system. On Windows and Mac, the first time you open the
app your computer may warn you that the app is not from a known publisher. The
desktop page shows what to press to continue.

If you use the web app, open it in your browser. There is nothing to install.

## 2. Sign in, or work without an account

The first screen is the sign-in screen. You can sign in in a few ways, and
[Your account](account.md) explains each one.

- Type your email and your password, then press **Sign in**. For a first visit,
  press **Create an account** instead.
- Press **Continue with Google**. Your browser opens, you pick your account, and
  you come back signed in.
- Type your email, then press **Email me a sign-in code** or **Email me a sign-in link instead**.

The desktop app also offers **Use without an account, local only**. Your work
then stays on your own computer. You need no sign-in and no internet connection.
If you see the small tag **Offline · on this computer** in the header, you are in
this mode. You can sign in later, and your local work stays where it is.

The first time you use an account on the server, you read a short privacy notice
once, then continue. [Your account](account.md) has the details.

## 3. Find your way around

The sidebar on the left lists the main screens. These are the ones you will use
first:

- **Home** is your dashboard. See [The dashboard](dashboard.md).
- **Papers** is your library of papers.
- **Notes** holds your notes.
- **Lists** groups papers by theme.

Later you will also meet **Log**, **Plan**, **Experiments** and **Graph**.

Your projects are not in the sidebar. Open **Projects** from your account menu,
which sits in the top bar behind your account button.

## 4. Make a project

A project holds the papers, notes and experiments of one piece of work, such as
a thesis. See [Projects and workspaces](workspaces.md) for more.

1. Open **Projects** from your account menu.
2. Select **New project**.
3. In **Name**, type a name, for example Latent Spaces Thesis.
4. Select **Create project**. The new project opens, and everything you add from
   now on goes into it.

## 5. Add a paper

1. Open **Papers**.
2. Select **Add paper**.
3. Under **…or import from**, choose **DOI**.
4. Paste a DOI, for example 10.1145/3592979. A doi.org link works too.
5. Select **Add paper**. The title, authors and year are filled in for you, and
   the paper appears in the list.

You can also add a paper from an arXiv id, a URL or a Zotero key. You can drop a
PDF on the form (**…or add from a PDF**), or import a whole BibTeX file
(**…or import a BibTeX file**). Dropping PDFs on the **Papers** list works too.

If the paper is already in your library, WeaveForge does not add it twice. It
tells you **Already in library**.

## 6. Write a note

1. Open **Notes**.
2. Select **New note**. An empty note opens in the editor.
3. Type a title on the first line, then write. Notes use Markdown, a simple way
   of marking headings, bold text and lists. [Notes](notes.md) covers the rest.
4. Your note saves as you type.

## 7. Find things again

1. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac). The search box opens, with the words
   **Jump to paper, note, or section…**.
2. Type part of a paper title or a note name.
3. Press Enter to open the top result, or Esc to close.

Ctrl+Shift+P (⌘⇧P) opens the same box with commands instead. Its prompt reads
**Run a command…**. See [Search](search.md).

## Stuck?

*The buttons on the sign-in screen are greyed out. Why?*
Every email option needs the **Email** box filled in first. Type your email, and
the buttons become active.

*I added a paper and nothing new appeared. Did it fail?*
Probably not. If the paper is already in your library, WeaveForge tells you
**Already in library** and does not add it again.

*The tag says Offline · on this computer. Is something wrong?*
No. You are in local mode. Your work stays on this computer, and nothing is sent
anywhere. See [The desktop and mobile apps](desktop.md#working-without-an-account).
