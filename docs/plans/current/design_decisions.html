C · The metric curve — smoothing, and one scale or many

chart

A loss curve exists to show a spike. The query layer in the code audit refuses to average for exactly that reason. Model 2 puts a smoothing slider in the UI.

A · No smoothing by default, one shared y-domain rec

Crosshair lists every visible series at the nearest step — both numbers, same axis. Free scale is an explicit opt-out, never the default.

Palette: take Model 2's Okabe–Ito / Set2 names, but verify each at 3:1 against both --surface and --surface2.
B · Model 2 — smoothing slider, independent scales
smooth

Step 60k–70k: the divergence a reader is looking for is not visible on either curve.

Against: a continuous smoothing control invites a researcher to smooth away the thing they are looking for. If smoothing ever ships, make it a named preset and label the axis.

E · One card shape, or two densities?

component

The card primitive already exists (entity-card.tsx, used in 8 files), so "enforce one shape" is largely already true. The question is whether a paper's own summary belongs behind a "Show more" disclosure — Model 1 says no, for text-first screens.

A · Today — one card, summary behind "Show more"

Attention is all you need

read
Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar et al. · 2017 · NeurIPS
#transformer#attention

Four of these fill the 760px screen. On a reading list, the researcher is reading their own titles back to themselves, and the status select is the most prominent control on the row.

Keep: the existing anatomy — header, meta, chips, foot — is right for experiments, milestones, report and shared, where the row genuinely is meta-first.

Already done — do not plan these

verified

Four claims in the audit are stale, and one is the largest single correction in the file:

Graph focus mode has shipped

graph-screen.tsx — useState(false), a graph-focus-btn, a full-bleed canvas. Both models and ui-spec §3.5 describe it as living on a branch.

A contrast gate exists and passes

npm run check:contrast — "0 normal-text pair(s) below AA". The thing D-06 asks for is built.

Fourteen themes, not eleven or seven

lib/theme/theme.ts — 6 light + 8 dark. Vivid and Pastel are in neither audit.

The card and empty-state primitives exist

entity-card.tsx in 8 files, empty-state.tsx in 13. The gap is the footer order and the loading/error half, not the primitive.
