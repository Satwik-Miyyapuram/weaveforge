The claim rests on a single coefficient. Higgins et al. put one hyperparameter in front of the KL term and show that raising it trades reconstruction for factorised latents.

“…a single hyperparameter β that balances latent channel capacity against reconstruction accuracy.”

Does β survive a structured prior?

Locatello argues unsupervised disentanglement is impossible without inductive bias — and a graph prior is exactly that bias. See Variational Graph Auto-Encoders.

Decision · 2026-02-11 Using the structured-prior variant rather than the β sweep for §3.2. The sweep was already run (β-VAE sweep — seed 42) and the ranking is unchanged, so re-running buys nothing.
